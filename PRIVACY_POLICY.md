# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the Meta Lead App handles information received through Meta Lead Ads for testing and demonstration purposes.

## Information We Collect

When a test lead is submitted through a Meta Lead Form, the application may receive information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Meta lead ID
- Form and page identifiers associated with the lead

## How We Use Information

The information received through the application is used only for:

- Testing the Meta Lead Ads integration
- Demonstrating lead retrieval and processing
- Displaying test leads in the application's dashboard
- Testing real-time lead notifications

## Data Storage

Test lead information may be stored in a local MySQL database used by the application during development and testing.

This application is a technical proof of concept and is not intended for production use.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or intentionally share submitted lead information with third parties.

## Data Retention

Test data may be deleted from the application's database at any time as part of development and testing.

## Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken during development to protect stored test data. However, this proof-of-concept application should not be considered a production-grade data storage system.

## Your Rights

If you have submitted information through a test form and want the test data removed, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with this application.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application changes during development.

## Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the application owner through the contact information provided in the Meta application settings.
